# AI & Robotic Automations 🤖

**Building intelligent systems that connect AI, software automation, and robotics.**

AI & Robotic Automations develops tools, experiments, and open-source technologies around artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, robotic automation, and intelligent workflows.

## 🚀 What We Build

Our work explores areas including:

* AI agents and autonomous workflows
* Robotic and process automation
* LLM-powered applications
* Machine learning and intelligent decision systems
* API and software integrations
* Developer automation tools
* Robotics and autonomous systems

Some of our technology is developed commercially and remains private, while selected tools, experiments, integrations, and research are released here as open source.

## 🌐 Open Source

We believe useful infrastructure and experimentation should be shared with the developer community.

Our public repositories include open-source tools, examples, research projects, integrations, and experimental technologies developed or maintained by AI & Robotic Automations.

⭐ If one of our projects helps you, consider starring the repository or contributing.
